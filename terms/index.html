1. The app

Shape & Shade uses your phone's camera, or a photo you choose, to map points on your face on your device, estimate your face shape from measurements, and, with Pro, suggest a color season and palette. It also offers style guides, a live color drape, a glasses try-on and saved scans.

2. For fun and style ideas

Color season results are for fun and style ideas and depend on lighting and your camera. Guides and suggestions are general style ideas, not professional advice.
